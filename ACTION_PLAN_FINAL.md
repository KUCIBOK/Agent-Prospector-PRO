## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

```
❌ Sending all 75 at once (too risky)
   └─ DO: Send in batches (5-15 per day)

❌ Generic subject lines
   └─ DO: Personalize with prospect name or detail

❌ Long emails (>250 words)
   └─ DO: Keep to 150-200 words

❌ Weak CTA ("Let me know if interested")
   └─ DO: Specific CTA ("Call Tuesday 2pm?")

❌ No follow-up plan
   └─ DO: Schedule follow-ups automatically

❌ Selling too hard in first email
   └─ DO: Focus on their problem, not your product

❌ No tracking
   └─ DO: Track every open, click, reply
```
